# Source review protocol amendment

The student authorised the assistant to conduct all benchmark, calibration-passage and answer reviews in the project conversation on 12 November 2026. This changes the original Proposal's human-checking arrangement. The 100-question design, grouped split, five configurations and development-only parameter selection remain the experimental design.

## Review provenance

New review records use `review_method=assistant_source_review` and identify the actual executing assistant in `reviewer`. Each decision retains its actual timestamp, original-source locator, reasoning and any revision. Benchmark review uses two source-reading passes before test execution. Original PDF pages and DOCX paragraphs or table rows are checked alongside indexed text. Passage relevance and answer support require semantic judgement on the requested facts, conditions, units and edition.

The same assistant system prepares and evaluates this benchmark. Two passes provide rechecking but do not establish independent human validation or expert agreement. Results will be described as assistant-reviewed observations. The final report must retain the resulting evaluation-bias and domain-expertise limitations. Student and supervisor approval fields refer only to actual approval events.

## Formal execution

Questions are revised before the study freeze. Development questions select parameters and support integration checks. Test outputs do not select weights or thresholds. Frozen inputs and actual first-attempt outputs are retained, including failures. Recovery observations retain distinct attempt identifiers and are reported separately.

The selected corpus currently has no verified same-family formal edition pair. Requests for unavailable years test corpus-relative handling. Where a request year has no verified formal edition, its status is explicitly recorded as an unavailable-year robustness scenario. Such cases support conclusions about edition constraints in this corpus.

## Budget accounting

Historical API spending is partly unrecorded. A historical budget reserve may be allocated while that spending remains unknown. The reserve is identified as a budget allocation. Known token usage, conservative remaining-call estimates, source prices and the £40 review against the £50 project allocation are retained in the execution record.

## Completion evidence

Research conclusions are derived from recorded outputs and review decisions. Portfolio entries, supervision approvals, the student's voice recording and school submission receipts retain their actual completion state.
